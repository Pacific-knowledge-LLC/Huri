Huri transforms files directly on your Mac and catalogs 305 archive, audio,
document, e-book, font, image, presentation, vector and video formats.

Convert images, documents, audio and video with settings tailored to each
format. Open-source engines installed on your Mac automatically extend the
available conversions. Use the PDF workshop to merge, reorder, rotate, extract
or split pages.

Huri works without an account, telemetry or file uploads. Your content stays
on your Mac, and the catalog clearly separates known formats from conversions
that are actually available.
